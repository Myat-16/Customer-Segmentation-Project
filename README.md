Customer Segmentation by Product Category & Country
Project Overview

This project applies K-Means Clustering and Pareto Analysis to segment customers based on purchasing behavior and identify key revenue-driving customer groups. The analysis uses three customer metrics:

Sales Volume
Order Count
Average Order Value (AOV)

The objective is to support data-driven business decisions by identifying high-value customers, growth opportunities, and effective resource allocation strategies. Based on the analysis, customers were grouped into four clusters with distinct purchasing patterns.

Key Findings
Customer Segmentation Results
Cluster 2 – High-Value Customers
Contributes approximately 64% of total sales.
Represents the most valuable customer segment.
Generates the largest share of revenue for the business.

Recommendation

Prioritize customer retention.
Implement loyalty programs and personalized promotions.
Offer premium products and exclusive benefits to maintain engagement and reduce churn.
Cluster 4 – Growth Potential Customers
Shows strong potential for future revenue growth.

Recommendation

Use targeted promotional campaigns.
Introduce product bundles and cross-selling strategies.
Encourage customers to increase spending and move into the high-value segment.
Cluster 3 – Regular Customers
Provides a stable and consistent revenue stream.

Recommendation

Create rewards and membership programs.
Run seasonal campaigns and personalized recommendations.
Increase customer lifetime value through repeat purchases.
Cluster 1 – Low-Value Customers
Represents the largest customer group but contributes the smallest share of total sales.

Recommendation

Apply cost-effective marketing strategies.
Use promotional offers and engagement campaigns.
Maintain marketing efficiency while encouraging additional purchases.
Pareto Analysis Insights

The Pareto analysis indicates that a relatively small group of customers is responsible for the majority of company revenue. Key findings include:

Cluster 2 contributes 64% of total sales.
Clusters 2, 3, and 4 collectively generate 95% of overall revenue.

These results demonstrate the importance of focusing business resources on retaining high-value customers while developing growth-potential segments.

Product Category Insights

The analysis identified the following top-performing product categories:

Dairy Products
Beverages

These categories present opportunities for:

Targeted promotions
Product bundle recommendations
Inventory optimization initiatives
Country-Level Opportunities

Customer activity across multiple countries suggests potential for:

Region-specific marketing campaigns
Localized product recommendations
Improved resource allocation based on market demand
Strategic Recommendations
Retain and reward high-value customers (Cluster 2).
Develop growth-potential customers (Cluster 4) through targeted promotions and cross-selling.
Increase engagement among regular customers (Cluster 3) with loyalty and rewards programs.
Use low-cost engagement strategies for low-value customers (Cluster 1).
Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-performing product categories.
Implement country-specific strategies to maximize market opportunities.
Conclusion

Customer segmentation and Pareto analysis reveal that business growth can be achieved by prioritizing high-value customer retention while nurturing growth-potential segments. By aligning marketing, sales, and operational strategies with customer behavior, the company can improve revenue generation, marketing efficiency, and long-term business sustainability.
